Teaching

I am a highly proficient accountant with a huge aptitude for multi-dimensional research in the fields of finance and accounting. I have a strong accounting background with a bachelor’s in accounting where I secured a 3.76 GPA and followed by a master's from prestigious Universitas Kristen Satya Wacana with a 4.00 GPA. I wish to advance my skill set further by brainstorming with academics.

I'm a lecturer in the accounting program at Universitas Kristen Satya Wacana. As a lecturer, my responsibilities include delivering lectures to bachelors students, conducting accounting research, and sharing my insights in knowledge groups.

I'm also a founder of @ke.olahdata which is a place for students all over the country to learn about data and process their research data with statistical tools like Excel, SPSS, Smart PLS, EVIEWS, and AMOS. I am a self-learner and highly optimistic. I consider myself a data evangelist, I see through opportunities from processing complex data sets and also hold the potential to convert them into tangible gains by discovering actionable and sustainable insights.
